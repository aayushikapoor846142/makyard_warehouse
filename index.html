Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Find answers to the most common questions about using Makyard.com.

How do I list my property?

Create a free account, click “Add Property”, fill in the property details, upload photos and publish your listing.

Is there a fee to list a property?

Basic listings are available with our packages. Choose a package that fits the number of properties, views and enquiries you need.

Can I contact the owner directly?

Yes. Every listing has Call and WhatsApp buttons so you can reach the owner directly.

Do you verify listings?

We review listings to help keep the marketplace trusted and secure, and we encourage buyers to visit a property before closing a deal.
